Problem Statement

There is a developer workflow challenge in both SDLC onboarding or debugging that IBM Bob resolves. Our team's deployment problem in the prototype solves several workflow challenges while reducing time and error reduction. Commercial space travel is accelerating rapidly, but it faces a massive existential hurdle. To the public, it often looks like an elite, carbon-heavy playground. If space tourism is going to survive and thrive, it must prove its value to Earth’s sustainability. Right now, there is a total disconnect between private space travelers and the profound impact their journeys could have on global sustainability goals. Modern pull request lifecycles stall because unit testing, linting/security triage, and release documentation are performed serially and manually by the engineer. Since IBM Bob explores untested boundary conditions, generates test suites, executes them via bash tool integration, and iteratively repairs broken tests without polluting other workflows, it's workflow solution evaluates modified files against coding standards and OWASP rules. Because IBM Bob runs in an isolated context sandbox, raw code chunks and intermediate ast traversals do not bloat the orchestrator's token budget during workflow instances.Due to our team's need to reframe this problem to a developer‑focused workflow, IBM Bob’s agents, parallel tasks, or sub‑agents were leveraged in our team's prototype.

That is why we built

EcoOrbit Steward 1.0: This app bridges space travel with direct, clean, and immediate green metrics that allow trusted NASA Data Stewards shares and positions ESG data sharing to the space tourist not just as a passenger, but as a "Steward" of both Earth and orbital space. We are the world’s first space tourism companion app designed to turn high-net-worth passengers into active stewards of human, space, and Earth ESG sustainability.

The Solution

Our platform leverages NASA’s open-source environmental data to weave sustainability directly into the passenger experience across three distinct pillars: Environmental (Earth Sustainability): We don’t just calculate flight carbon footprints; we actively offset them. Through the app, a portion of every ticket is dynamically routed to NASA-vetted, satellite-monitored carbon capture and reforestation projects on Earth. Furthermore, when tourists are in orbit, the app alerts them to look at environmental crisis zones—like deforestation in the Amazon or receding glacial lines—using space to create a profound "Overview Effect" that drives real-world philanthropy. Social (Human Sustainability & Inclusion): Space belongs to all of humanity. Our "Space for All" governance initiative mandates that private providers using our app fund STEM scholarship programs for underrepresented communities on Earth. The app features an interactive portal where tourists can livestream educational Q&A sessions directly from orbit to classrooms worldwide, democratizing the inspiration of spaceflight. Governance (Space Sustainability): Orbital debris is a looming disaster. Our app enforces strict Space ESG compliance. It tracks the specific flight’s orbital path using live telemetry and logs zero-waste data practices aboard the vehicle, ensuring operators adhere to international space debris mitigation guidelines.
